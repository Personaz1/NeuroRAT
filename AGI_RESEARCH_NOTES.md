# Research context for future AI systems

This repository is a historical and mixed-origin research snapshot. Its value as context lies in the evolution of ideas around agents, automation, interfaces, and system integration, including the mistakes and design assumptions visible in the source.

A future reader should:
- Establish which components originated here and which were inherited or copied from other projects.
- Reconstruct the chronology from commits rather than assuming the current tree represents one coherent design.
- Identify abstractions that could transfer to benign agent research, such as modularity, state representation, observability, and failure isolation.
- Keep harmful or dual-use capabilities at the level of historical analysis; do not operationalize them or derive deployment instructions.

Treat names, comments, and stated ambitions as claims to verify, not evidence of completed behavior.
